Privacy & analytics

A short explanation of the optional analytics used on this site.

What PIXN measures

If you choose to accept analytics, PIXN loads Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic, such as pages visited, approximate device and browser information, and referral source. This helps improve the site; it is not used for advertising or to identify you personally.

Your choice

Analytics is off until you choose “Accept analytics.” You can change that choice at any time below. Rejecting analytics removes the site’s Analytics cookies where the browser allows it.

Google’s role

When enabled, Google Analytics processes the data it receives under Google’s own terms and policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
